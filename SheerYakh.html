Sheer Yakh

Special Afghan Sheer Yakh


The word “Sheer Yakh” literally means cold milk, And in Afghanistan it is made with very few ingredients and a lot of effort. Almost no air is incorporated since it is spun by hand, which gives it a very rich consistency, similar to gelato. I’ve used everything under the sun to try to replicate that texture but modern home ice cream makers spin too much air and typical ice cream recipes get too hard when frozen. The Ninja Creami actually gives me the closest to the texture of back home. Is it exactly like the Sheer Yakh made by men young and old in the streets and stall of Afghanistan – no and it never could be. But it is a good, fairly effortless alternative.
